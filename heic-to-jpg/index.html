What HEIC actually is

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the still-image format Apple introduced in iOS 11 in 2017 and has been the default camera output on every iPhone and iPad since. HEIC is a wrapper around HEVC (H.265) video compression, which is why it produces files roughly half the size of an equivalent JPG with the same visible quality. For iPhone storage and iCloud Photos, that's a meaningful saving. For everyone else, it's a compatibility headache.

The headache exists because HEIC is patent-encumbered. Apple ships native HEIC support throughout iOS and macOS, but Windows requires a Microsoft Store extension, most Linux distros need a libheif install, web browsers have no native HEIC decoder at all, and many older versions of design tools (Photoshop, GIMP, Figma) cannot open HEIC. So the moment you need to share an iPhone photo with someone outside the Apple ecosystem — email it to a colleague, attach it to a forum post, upload it to a website — you typically have to convert.

Why convert HEIC to JPG specifically

JPG is the universally compatible photographic format. It's supported by every operating system, every web browser, every email client, every social network, every printer driver. If you've ever asked yourself "will this image open on the other person's computer?" — JPG is the answer that's been "yes" since 1993. For sharing iPhone photos with non-Apple users, JPG is the safest target.

The trade-off is file size: an HEIC photo at 3 MB will typically expand to a 4–6 MB JPG at quality 90, or stay around 2–3 MB at quality 82. imgready defaults to quality 82, which produces a JPG visually indistinguishable from the HEIC at typical viewing distance and roughly the same file size.

How imgready decodes HEIC

imgready loads the libheif-js library (a WebAssembly compile of the canonical libheif C library) on demand the first time you drop a HEIC file. The library is approximately 1.5 MB; it's downloaded once from a public CDN and cached by your browser for all subsequent files in your session. The decoder then reads the HEVC-compressed pixel data, decodes it into raw RGBA, and hands it to imgready's normal pipeline for re-encoding into JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, or GIF. The decode step happens entirely in your browser tab — your photos are never sent to any server.

Multiple HEIC files? Use batch mode

If you've just exported a year of photos from iCloud and want to convert them all to JPG for an external hard drive backup, drop the entire folder into imgready and click "Process All." Each HEIC is decoded and re-encoded sequentially. When all files are done, click "Download All" to get a single ZIP. The original HEIC files on your computer are not touched — imgready creates separate JPG copies.

When to prefer PNG or WebP instead of JPG

  • If you need pixel-perfect quality (archival, professional photography), pick PNG. Lossless. The downside is much larger files.
  • If you're going to publish on the web (your blog, your portfolio, an Etsy listing), pick WebP — typically 25–35% smaller than the equivalent JPG with the same visible quality.
  • If you're emailing the photo or attaching it to anything that might still struggle with WebP, stick with JPG.
Privacy note: iPhone photos carry EXIF metadata including the GPS coordinates of where they were taken. By default, imgready strips this on conversion (you can opt back in via Settings → Metadata). If you're sharing a photo publicly, leaving GPS in is a privacy risk worth thinking about.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I open HEIC files on Windows?

HEIC is an Apple-developed format that requires specific decoder support. Windows 10 and 11 require the HEIF Image Extensions (free) and HEVC Video Extensions (paid) from the Microsoft Store. imgready sidesteps the issue entirely by decoding HEIC in your browser.

Is HEIC conversion private?

Yes. The libheif decoder library is downloaded from a public CDN, but your actual photos never leave your device. All decoding and re-encoding happens locally in your browser's memory.

How big is the libheif decoder download?

About 1.5 MB. It's downloaded once on your first HEIC conversion and cached for the rest of the session — subsequent HEIC files decode instantly.

Can imgready convert HEIC in batch?

Yes. Drop dozens or hundreds of HEIC files at once and click Process. The soft cap is 200 files; the practical limit is your device's memory.

Does converting HEIC to JPG reduce quality?

Slightly. HEIC is a lossy format and JPG is a lossy format, so the conversion is technically a second round of compression. At imgready's default quality 82, the difference is invisible at normal viewing distance. If you need pixel-perfect output, convert to PNG instead — it's lossless but the file will be larger.

Can imgready handle HEIC bursts and Live Photos?

imgready decodes the still-image component of HEIC files. Live Photos store an additional MOV video alongside the still — imgready only converts the still image; the video portion is not extracted.
