Why use SVG patterns?

Raster backgrounds (like large JPG or PNG wallpapers) take hundreds of kilobytes or even megabytes of bandwidth. In contrast, an SVG pattern definition is typically under 1 kilobyte. The browser tiles the mathematical formula across any viewport size instantly, with crisp lines that never blur on high-density Retina or 4K displays.

How do I use the CSS in my website?

Is the 4K PNG print-ready?

Yes! The 4K export renders a 4096×4096 pixel canvas tile, which is suitable for high-density 300 DPI physical printing (apparel, fabrics, wallpapers, stationery).
