imgready

Free image optimization, right in your browser

imgready is a fast, private, and free tool for compressing and converting images. Unlike other image optimization services, imgready processes everything locally in your browser — your files never leave your device, and there are no upload limits, file size caps, or batch restrictions of any kind.

Your privacy, guaranteed. No uploads. No servers. No accounts required. Every image is processed using your browser's built-in capabilities and WebAssembly encoders, then discarded from memory when you're done. Not even the developer can see your files.

Why imgready exists

I built imgready out of genuine frustration. As a web developer and designer, handling image files is part of my daily workflow — resizing screenshots, converting iPhone HEIC photos, batch-optimizing product images for client sites. Most of the tools I tried were either limited to a handful of files, capped file sizes at 5 MB, couldn't handle HEIC at all, or required an account just to download your own compressed image.

I wanted something that handled everything: any format, any file size, any number of images, with no friction. When I couldn't find it, I built it. imgready is my first app, and it's built to solve a real problem I ran into every single week.

Supported formats

imgready accepts JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF (iPhone and iPad photos), TIFF (professional photography and print), BMP (legacy bitmap), and SVG (scalable vector graphics). It handles formats that most online tools cannot — including HEIC decoding using a specialized JavaScript library and TIFF parsing with support for uncompressed, LZW, and PackBits compression.

Output formats include WebP, AVIF, JPEG, PNG (lossless at quality 100, or lossy PNG-8 via color quantization below 100), and GIF.

How it works under the hood

imgready uses the HTML5 Canvas API and WebAssembly encoders to read, process, and re-encode your images entirely within the browser. For formats like HEIC that browsers don't natively support, imgready loads a lightweight decoder library on demand — about 1.5 MB, downloaded once. For modern output formats like AVIF and WebP, imgready uses jsquash, a set of high-quality WASM encoders that work consistently across all devices and operating systems. All encoding, decoding, resizing, and format conversion happens in your browser's memory. Nothing is sent over the network.

Who imgready is for

imgready was built for anyone who works with images regularly and needs a tool that gets out of the way. That includes web developers optimizing images for faster page loads, designers converting between formats for client deliverables, bloggers and content creators preparing photos for publishing, social media managers converting iPhone HEIC photos to shareable formats, and anyone who values privacy and would rather not upload personal photos to a third-party server just to resize them.

Free, always — and why

imgready is completely free with no feature limits. There are no paywalls, no "pro" tiers for basic functionality, and no plans to add them. The tool is kept running by optional contributions from people who find it useful, alongside non-intrusive display advertising. If you want to remove the "Optimized by imgready" badge from the SDK for use in your own projects, there are one-time license keys available on the developer page — but the core tool itself will always be free for everyone.

If imgready has saved you time, a small contribution on Ko-fi goes a long way toward keeping it free and actively maintained. Every feature is free for everyone — your support simply helps keep the lights on ♥

What imgready handles

Supported input formats include JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, BMP, GIF, and SVG. Output formats include JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF. The tool handles files of any size — there is no per-file or per-session cap — and can process multiple files at once via batch mode.

Browser compatibility

imgready works in any modern browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari (desktop and mobile), and Edge. iOS Safari is fully supported, including HEIC conversion on iPhone. No extensions, plugins, or software downloads are required.

Frequently asked questions

Is imgready completely free?

Yes — imgready is free to use with no limits, no feature paywalls, and no accounts required. It runs on Google AdSense ads and optional Ko-fi contributions.

Who built imgready?

imgready was built by Jeffrey, a web developer and designer based in California. It started as a personal tool and grew into something useful enough to share.

Does imgready work offline?

Once the page has loaded, imgready works without a network connection for formats that don't require the HEIC or AVIF WebAssembly decoders. Those libraries (~1.5 MB each) are cached after first use.

Is there a mobile app?

No dedicated app — imgready works in any modern mobile browser. Add it to your home screen for quick access. On iOS, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Can I convert TIFF and other professional formats?

Yes. imgready accepts TIFF (with uncompressed, LZW, and PackBits compression), SVG, BMP, and GIF in addition to JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, and HEIC. It's designed to handle formats that most online tools cannot.

Does imgready preserve metadata like EXIF data?

No — imgready strips all metadata including EXIF, IPTC, and XMP when converting images. This protects your privacy by removing location, camera settings, and other embedded information from your photos before download.
