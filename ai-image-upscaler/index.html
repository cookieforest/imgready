Bigger, without the blur

Stretching an image in an editor makes every pixel bigger and nothing sharper. This uses a neural network, trained on pairs of sharp and degraded photos, to rebuild the detail a larger version would have had: fabric weave, hair, foliage, the grain in powder-coated metal.

It does not invent new objects. The models here were picked because they stay close to the original rather than painting in texture that was never there.

Fast or best

Fast runs a small model (about 1 MB) that handles phone photos and compressed JPGs well and finishes in seconds. Best runs a much larger transformer model (about 49 MB) that recovers noticeably more fine detail. It is worth it for product photos and anything going to print, as long as you have a minute and a recent graphics card.

We compared eleven upscalers on 18 real photos by shrinking each one, enlarging it again and measuring how close it came back to the original. Best came out on top for clean images and for compressed ones. Fast was the best of the quick models.

Nothing is uploaded

Most AI upscalers send your photo to a server and charge credits for the GPU time. Here the model downloads to your browser once, then runs on your own graphics card through WebGPU. Open the Network tab while it works: after the model, no request carries your image.

Common questions

How much bigger can it make an image?

2x or 4x on each side, so 4 or 16 times the pixels. The output is capped at about 48 megapixels on computers and 16 on iPhone and iPad, because past that the browser runs out of memory.

Why does Best take so long?

It is a transformer model with about 11 million parameters, running in your browser rather than on a data-centre GPU. On a recent graphics card expect roughly a minute per megapixel of input. The model downloads once and stays cached.

Does it work on a phone?

Fast does, on phones with WebGPU (recent Android Chrome and iOS 26 Safari). Best is heavy for a phone; a laptop or desktop is the better place for it.

Will it fix a blurry photo?

It helps with softness from compression and small size. It cannot recover a photo that is badly out of focus or motion blurred, and it will not add detail that was never captured.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The model file is downloaded from this site, then everything runs in this tab. Your image never leaves your device.
