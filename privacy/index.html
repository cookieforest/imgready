Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026 · Operator: imgready (sole proprietorship operated by Jeffrey, California, USA) · Contact: hello@imgready.app

The short version. imgready is a browser-based tool. Your image files are processed entirely on your own device and never uploaded to our servers. We do not run accounts, do not collect names, addresses or payment information, and the only personal-data processors we share information with are Google AdSense (for ads), Cloudflare (for hosting and the CDN), and a small number of CDNs that serve our compression libraries.

1. Who we are (data controller)

The data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the controller/business for the California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA (CCPA) is the operator of imgready.app, contactable at hello@imgready.app. We do not have an EU representative; if you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland and prefer to contact us by post, email us first and we will provide a postal address.

2. What we process and why

2.1 Image files you load into the tool

When you drop, paste, or select an image file inside imgready, that file is decoded, processed and re-encoded entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript, WebAssembly and the HTML5 Canvas API. The file is held in your browser's RAM only for as long as you keep the tab open. It is never transmitted to imgready.app, Cloudflare, Google, or any other party. When you close or refresh the page, all image data is discarded.

Because we never receive your image files, we cannot delete, export or copy them on your behalf — there is nothing on our side to act on. You can clear them yourself by clicking "Clear All" or closing the tab.

2.2 Server logs (Cloudflare)

imgready is hosted on Cloudflare. When your browser requests a page, Cloudflare receives standard HTTP request information (IP address, user agent, requested URL, referrer, response code) and processes it briefly to deliver the response, defend against attacks and surface basic analytics. Cloudflare's processing is governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Lawful basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest in operating, securing and analysing the service.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdSense)

imgready is supported by display advertising provided by Google AdSense. AdSense and its partners may set cookies, read existing cookies, and use device identifiers to serve, measure, and personalise ads. The categories of data Google may process include: IP address, approximate location, user agent, ad interactions, and pseudonymous identifiers tied to ad cookies. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy & Terms — partner sites page and the Google Ads policy.

You can review and change your ad personalisation choices at any time via the "Cookie & ad settings" link in the footer of every page, or directly at Google Ads Settings.

2.4 Compression libraries (CDN)

To keep imgready fast, certain compression libraries are loaded on demand from public CDNs. These CDNs receive your IP address and user agent as part of fetching the file, the same way any web request does. We pin specific versions, do not pass any image content to these CDNs, and only load each library when you use a feature that needs it.

4. Consent (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, on your first visit you are shown a consent banner provided by a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (Google Funding Choices). You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies — including those used for ad personalisation — and change your choice any time via the "Cookie & ad settings" link in the footer. We rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for non-essential cookies and on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for essential security and CDN logging.

5. California / CPRA — Do Not Sell or Share

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, the use of advertising cookies on imgready may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. California residents (and visitors who send a Global Privacy Control signal) can opt out at any time by clicking "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the footer of every page, or by enabling Global Privacy Control in their browser, which we honour automatically.

You also have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access and request deletion of personal information we hold about you, to correct inaccuracies, and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of these rights. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@imgready.app with the subject "CCPA request". We will verify your request and respond within 45 days.

6. EU/UK rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland you have the right to: access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, and data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. Because we hold no account data and never receive your image files, the practical scope of these rights is limited to advertising / measurement cookies — and you can exercise those rights through Google's controls and the consent banner described above. For anything else, email hello@imgready.app.

7. Data retention

We do not store your image files at any point. Cloudflare retains its standard server logs for up to 30 days. Google AdSense applies its own retention rules described in Google's ads policy. The localStorage values we set on your browser remain until you clear them yourself.

8. International transfers

imgready operates from the United States. Cloudflare and Google process data in the US, the EU and other regions; both rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses for cross-border transfers from the EEA and UK to the US.

9. Children's privacy

imgready is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has interacted with our advertising partners through our site, contact us and we will work with the relevant partner to remove the data.

10. Security

imgready is served over HTTPS only, sets HSTS, and loads its compression libraries from pinned versions on reputable CDNs. Because we never receive your image files, the most common image-tool risk — server-side breach of uploaded files — does not apply.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post a notice at the top of this page and update the "Last updated" date if we make material changes. The current version is always at imgready.app/privacy/.
