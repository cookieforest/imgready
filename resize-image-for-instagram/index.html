Portrait vs landscape: which works best

Square (1:1, 1080×1080): Classic feed post. Shows full-screen on most phones. Good for product photos, flat lays, and graphics. Fills the feed without borders.

Portrait (4:5, 1080×1350): Most common engagement format. Slightly taller than square. Better for Carousel posts and tall portraits. Covers more screen real estate.

Landscape (1.91:1, 1080×566): Widest format for feed. Good for panoramas and wide scenes. Less common; reserve for landscape photos or panoramas where cropping would damage composition.

Stories and Reels (9:16, 1080×1920): Full-screen vertical. Use for behind-the-scenes, quick updates, and short video content. Most engaging format on mobile.

How to keep quality after resizing

When resizing, quality can suffer if you're shrinking a low-resolution image or enlarging a high-resolution one. Best practice: start with the largest image you have (from a high-resolution camera or smartphone), then resize down to Instagram dimensions. Resizing down always preserves quality better than resizing up.

If your image is smaller than 1080px wide, don't enlarge it — upload as-is. Instagram will enlarge it, which may look softer, but at least you tried. Always start with the best source material possible.

Frequently asked questions

What size should Instagram photos be?

Instagram feed photos should be 1080×1080 for square, 1080×1350 for portrait, and 1080×566 for landscape. Stories and Reels are 1080×1920. Always resize to the exact dimensions first to avoid Instagram cropping your image. Max file size is 8 MB for static images.

What is the best resolution for Instagram?

1080px width is the standard across all Instagram content. This displays at full quality on modern smartphone and tablet screens. Don't upload larger than 1080px width — Instagram will resize anyway, and the larger file wastes data on mobile. Resize to 1080px, then compress.

Does Instagram compress images?

Yes, Instagram re-encodes and compresses every image you upload, converting to JPEG format. Your original format doesn't matter — it will be compressed regardless. Pre-resizing to 1080px and pre-compressing reduces upload time and saves phone storage, but doesn't affect final quality much.

What format does Instagram prefer?

Instagram accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, and more, but converts everything to JPEG for storage. Use JPEG for photos (more efficient) and PNG for graphics with transparency. Instagram will re-compress both anyway, so upload quality doesn't matter much — start with the best source image possible.

Can I post PNG to Instagram?

Yes, Instagram accepts PNG files. Use PNG for logos, graphics, or images with transparency. However, PNG files are larger than JPEG, so Instagram may compress more aggressively. For photographs, JPEG is more efficient. Instagram converts PNGs to JPEG during processing anyway.

How do I avoid Instagram cropping my photo?

Use the exact aspect ratios: 1:1 for square, 4:5 for portrait, 1.91:1 for landscape, 9:16 for Stories/Reels. Resize to the exact dimensions (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×566, 1080×1920) using imgready, then preview in Instagram's crop preview before posting. Exact dimensions = no surprises.
