Why it costs anything

The free image libraries stop exactly where the pain starts. The most widely used one accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP and BMP, and no browser outside Safari decodes HEIC on its own. So the format every iPhone produces is the one nothing handles, and making it work took a compiled decoder, a fallback chain, and a lot of testing against real phone output.

That is what the licence pays for. Not compression, which is a solved problem you could wire up yourself in an afternoon.

Yes, you can read the source

It ships as plain JavaScript and WebAssembly. Read it, self-host it, audit it, diff it between versions. A tool whose entire claim is that nothing leaves your device has no business shipping an obfuscated blob you cannot inspect.

So the licence is a legal agreement, not a lock, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. What enforces it is that the attribution badge shows on your own site. If you are shipping a product to paying customers, you already know whether you are licensed.

Before you buy

What counts as one product?

One application or site that you own, on any number of domains you control, including staging and preview environments. A second product your company ships needs Unlimited, as does anything you build and hand to a client.

Is it per developer or per company?

Per company. There are no seats to count and nothing to install per machine. Everyone at your company can work on the licensed product.

What happens after twelve months?

Nothing breaks. The licence is perpetual, so the version you have keeps working and stays licensed forever. You stop receiving new releases until you renew, and renewing is optional.

I bought a $9, $29 or $99 key. Is it still valid?

Yes, permanently, on the terms you bought it under. Those tiers are retired and no longer sold, but the keys keep working and keep the badge off. You do not need to buy again.

Do you offer refunds?

Within 30 days, for any reason, no questions. The SDK is free to try with the badge on, so you can confirm it works on your own files before paying.

Will the web tool start charging?

No. imgready.app stays free, with no account, no cap and no watermark. The SDK is what funds it.
